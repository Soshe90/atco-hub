Opening the app…

Add this page to your home screen. The icon always finds the app, even after its address changes.

  1. In Safari, tap the Share button.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. If you see Open as Web App, turn it off.
  4. Tap Add, then open ATCO Hub from the new icon.
